Refugee Hub / Global Refugee Sponsorship Initiative

Instructional Designer

Six-month design engagement · January–July 2019

During a defined six-month engagement, I worked as Instructional Designer alongside an IRCC content specialist and supported UNHCR and IRCC stakeholder review. I analysed detailed sponsorship requirements, translated them into learner actions, and designed the relationship between self-directed online preparation and facilitator-led workshops. My responsibilities covered course structure, scenarios, knowledge checks, visual explanations, learner materials, workshop plans and facilitator guidance, followed by accuracy, coherence and usability review. The complete online, workshop, learner and facilitator package was organized for handoff and prepared for pilot delivery. This role was bounded in timeframe and scope, and the portfolio does not claim post-pilot outcomes.

  • Analyse requirements with an IRCC content specialist
  • Design connected online and facilitator-led learning
  • Develop learner, workshop and facilitator resources for coordinated pilot handoff
  • Support UNHCR and IRCC review before pilot

Selected work example

Online sponsorship preparation

Prospective sponsors needed to interpret detailed requirements before discussing how they applied to unfamiliar situations. I organized the online learning around a visible four-stage pathway, then added scenarios and immediate knowledge checks that translated guidance into decisions. Content-specialist and stakeholder review checked accuracy, clarity and readiness for the wider pilot package.

Sponsor training workshops
