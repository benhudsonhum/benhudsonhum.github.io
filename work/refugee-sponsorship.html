Refugee Hub / Global Refugee Sponsorship Initiative

Blended refugee-sponsorship training

During a defined six-month engagement, I worked with an IRCC content specialist to turn complex sponsorship requirements into online preparation, facilitated application, scenarios and practical resources prepared for pilot delivery.

Role
Instructional designer
Audience
Refugee sponsors and sponsor trainers
Timeframe
Six-month design engagement
Principal outputs
Online, workshop, learner and facilitator resources

Visual story

From requirements to sponsor decisions.

On smaller screens, swipe or scroll through the folio. With the folio focused, use the arrow keys.

The brief

Prospective sponsors needed to interpret detailed requirements and apply them to unfamiliar, human situations. A single information-heavy course would not provide enough opportunity to discuss roles, negotiate judgement or prepare a group to act together. The learning also needed to distinguish policy guidance from decisions that depended on context.

The engagement therefore connected self-directed preparation with facilitated application. I worked with an IRCC content specialist to define learner actions and structure the material, while UNHCR and IRCC stakeholders reviewed the developing package before pilot delivery. Online and workshop elements needed to function as one sequence rather than parallel resources.

Requirements to pilot package

What I designed and delivered

Translated requirements into actions

I worked with the content specialist to identify what sponsors needed to understand, decide and coordinate at each stage of the sponsorship pathway.

Connected two learning modes

I designed online preparation, scenarios and knowledge checks to feed directly into workshop discussion, group roles and facilitated application.

Built a complete resource set

I developed the online structure alongside workshop plans, learner activities, visual explanations and detailed facilitator guidance for consistent delivery.

Prepared for pilot delivery

I incorporated UNHCR and IRCC stakeholder review, checked policy alignment, learning coherence and usability, and packaged materials for facilitators and learners.

What the work produced

Review focused on policy accuracy, learning alignment, facilitator usability and coherence between online and workshop components. Revision also checked that workshop prompts and facilitator notes remained connected to the same learner actions. The resulting package was organized for handoff and prepared for pilot delivery; this portfolio does not claim post-pilot outcomes.
