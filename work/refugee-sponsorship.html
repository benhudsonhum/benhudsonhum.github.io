Refugee Hub / Global Refugee Sponsorship Initiative

Instructional Designer

Six-month design engagement · January–July 2019

During a six-month engagement, I adapted participatory instructional principles developed through my Theatre for a Change work to the distinct needs of community sponsorship. I combined those principles with a specific needs analysis, the expertise of an IRCC content specialist and UNHCR/IRCC stakeholder review. Together, these inputs shaped a connected pathway of self-directed online preparation and facilitator-led workshops for sponsors and sponsor trainers. My role included course structure, knowledge, attitude and skill outcomes, scenarios, knowledge checks, visual explanations, learner materials, workshop plans and facilitator guidance. I designed for information access, authentic judgement, reflection, practical problem-solving and preparation for independent action, then supported accuracy, coherence, accessibility and usability review across the package. Subject-matter authority remained with the IRCC specialist and reviewing partners; my engagement concluded with a complete package prepared for pilot delivery.

  • Combine needs analysis with IRCC subject-matter expertise
  • Adapt participatory principles to community-sponsorship learning
  • Design connected online, workshop and facilitator resources
  • Support UNHCR and IRCC review before pilot handoff

Online sponsorship preparation

The online course gave prospective sponsors a four-stage route through detailed requirements, scenarios and immediate decisions. I used information access, authentic judgement and reflection to prepare learners for later workshop discussion and independent action. The pathway was shaped by the needs analysis, IRCC content expertise and stakeholder review rather than treating online content as a stand-alone information repository.
