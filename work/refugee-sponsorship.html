Refugee Hub / GRSI

Training for refugee sponsorship groups

Refugee Hub / Global Refugee Sponsorship Initiative · Instructional Designer · January–July 2019

During a six-month engagement, I used needs analysis and participatory design to connect online preparation with facilitator-led sponsorship training.

An in-person sponsorship training manual

I worked with an IRCC subject-matter specialist to turn policy and procedure into outcomes, scenarios, decisions, visual explanations and trainer guidance, then incorporated review from UNHCR and IRCC stakeholders before pilot handoff.

Six complete manual pages show a facilitator-led sequence moving from overall structure through group roles, collaborative practice, communication scenarios and reflective facilitation.
