Refugee Hub / Global Refugee Sponsorship Initiative

Instructional Designer

Six-month design engagement · January–July 2019

During a six-month engagement with the Refugee Hub and Global Refugee Sponsorship Initiative, I designed blended learning that translated complex sponsorship policy and procedure into practical preparation for sponsors and sponsor trainers. I combined a needs analysis and participatory principles from my earlier work with the subject-matter expertise of an IRCC specialist and review from UNHCR and IRCC stakeholders. My contribution included the connected online and workshop pathway, knowledge, attitude and skill outcomes, scenarios, knowledge checks, visual explanations, learner materials and facilitator guidance, together with review for accuracy, accessibility, usability and coherence before pilot handoff. Subject-matter authority remained with the IRCC specialist and reviewing partners.

  • Ground the design in needs analysis and IRCC subject-matter expertise
  • Adapt participatory learning to community sponsorship
  • Connect online preparation, workshops and facilitator support
  • Use UNHCR and IRCC review to refine the package before pilot handoff
01

Selected work example

Online resource architecture

The online resource complemented facilitated training rather than duplicating it. A clear entry point introduced the package, while the four-stage overview made inquiry, planning, settlement support and group considerations visible so sponsors and trainers could locate guidance before, during and after workshops.

02

Selected work example

Scenario-based decisions and reflection

Sponsors encountered guidance as decisions rather than fixed answers. Knowledge checks tested practical judgement, sorting activities surfaced assumptions, and expandable scenarios asked groups to compare risks, responsibilities and possible responses before facilitated group discussion and practical planning.

03

Selected work example

Adapting participatory learning for sponsor workshops

The online course and workshops were designed as one learning pathway. Online preparation established a shared base of information; facilitated sessions then asked sponsors to interpret that guidance together, examine power and privacy, test strategies and plan practical action. I adapted participatory principles with the IRCC content specialist and wrote sponsor-trainer guidance that supported sound judgement without prescribing one answer for every context.
