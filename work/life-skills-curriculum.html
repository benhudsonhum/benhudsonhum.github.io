Theatre for a Change

Participatory curricula and facilitator training

Theatre for a Change · Curriculum, Training and Assessment Advisor · 2014–2016 · later freelance instructional designer and curriculum developer

At Theatre for a Change, I developed and codified a participatory methodology, then designed curricula and resources around knowledge, attitude and skill outcomes. I turned those outcomes into modelled activities, learner practice, reflection and facilitator guidance across Malawi and Southern Africa. As an advisor and later freelance designer, I developed facilitators through demonstration, observation, rehearsal and feedback so the method could be adapted without losing its learning purpose.
