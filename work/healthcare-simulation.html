A self-directed ADHD diagnostic-interview course

The Brightspace course gave learners a clear pathway through specialist explanation, accessible learning materials and custom webpage interactions before they applied that knowledge in interview decisions.

Design, delivery and evaluation

Working with clinicians, researchers, instructors, actors and technical partners, I translated intended interview performance into explanation, guided decisions, repeated practice, assessment and feedback, while supporting Brightspace delivery and evaluation. Clinical experts retained authority for clinical content; the learning design made that expertise usable in realistic interview practice.

Alongside this interactive practice, I designed, coordinated and produced 48 actor-based simulations for repeated diagnostic-interview practice, structured observation and feedback. I worked with uOttawa to develop and host the course website and coordinated a research project comparing the self-directed and instructor-led programmes with 50 undergraduate students.
