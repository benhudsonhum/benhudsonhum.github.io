CHEO Research Institute · Healthcare professional education

Research Associate / Instructional Designer

2020–present

At CHEO Research Institute, I work with clinicians and healthcare researchers to design education for healthcare professionals completing a complex diagnostic-interview task. I begin with the clinical and educational purpose, learner context and existing instruction across varied delivery contexts and formats, then help define intended interview performance, meaningful outcomes and evaluation questions. Clinical experts retain authority for clinical content; I align their expertise with explanations, examples, decision practice, facilitated actor-based simulation, feedback and assessment. My role spans instructor-led, webinar and self-directed learning, multimedia production, Brightspace implementation and QA, accessible support, research and formal evaluation. I use learner, instructor, technical and implementation evidence to refine the pathway while preserving what already works. Across this role, I have designed 48 actor-based video simulations; the portfolio describes the evaluation process without presenting unpublished findings.

  • Inquire into clinical purpose, learners, context and existing instruction
  • Define interview performance, outcomes and evaluation questions
  • Align explanation, decisions, simulation, feedback and assessment
  • Build and refine multimedia, Brightspace delivery and QA from evidence

Learner feedback, accessibility checks, completion logic and technical QA informed documented revision before evaluation.
