CHEO

ADHD diagnostic-interview training for healthcare professionals

CHEO Research Institute · Research Associate / Instructional Designer · 2020–present

I designed a structured ADHD diagnostic-interview programme for healthcare professionals across self-directed study, live webinars and actor-based simulation. Working with clinicians, researchers, instructors, actors and technical partners, I translated intended interview performance into explanation, guided decisions, repeated practice, assessment and feedback, while supporting Brightspace delivery and evaluation. Clinical experts retained authority for clinical content; the learning design made that expertise usable in realistic interview practice.

01

A self-directed ADHD diagnostic-interview course

The Brightspace course gave learners a visible route through specialist explanation, searchable rating guidance and selectable history content before they applied that knowledge in interview decisions.

02

Interactive interview practice

Learners observed realistic interviews, chose questions or ratings and reviewed the reasoning behind their decisions before moving into further practice and feedback.

Questioning and Rating Practice

Interactive video in which learners practise choosing effective questions and assigning ratings from interview evidence.

Informant Problem Solving

Interactive video focused on responding to challenges that arise while interviewing an informant.

An interview excerpt introduces a common information-gathering problem, followed by a learner decision and written reasoning that connects the observation to a better next step.
