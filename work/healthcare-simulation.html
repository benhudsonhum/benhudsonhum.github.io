CHEO Research Institute · Healthcare professional education

Research Associate / Instructional Designer

2020–present

At CHEO Research Institute, I lead the design, development and evaluation of ADHD diagnostic-interview learning for healthcare professionals, including 48 actor-based video simulations. I work with clinicians, researchers, instructors, actors and technical partners to define the educational purpose, intended interview performance and evaluation questions; clinical experts retain authority for clinical content. I translate that expertise into explanation, structured practice, simulation, assessment and feedback across instructor-led, webinar and self-directed formats, while coordinating plans, review cycles, production, Brightspace implementation, quality assurance and delivery support. I use learner, instructor, technical and evaluation evidence to guide revision; this portfolio describes the research design rather than unpublished findings.

  • Clarify the clinical purpose, learner context and existing strengths
  • Define interview performance, outcomes and evaluation questions
  • Align explanation, practice, simulation, assessment and feedback
  • Coordinate production, Brightspace implementation, QA, evaluation and revision
01

Selected work example

Self-directed ADHD diagnostic-interview course

The self-directed course had to preserve the explanation, guided reasoning and active practice available in live training without simply recording the live programme. Working with clinicians and researchers, I mapped the interview task and designed chaptered media, interactive reference content, assessment and decision-based practice in Brightspace. Learner feedback, accessibility review, completion logic and technical QA informed a documented revision process before formal evaluation.

Selected work example

Instructor-led training, simulation and evaluation

The facilitated programme centred on repeated interview practice with actors, structured observation and feedback. Working with clinicians, researchers and actors, I translated the interview into scenarios, performance guidance, rating criteria and debrief structures, then coordinated multimedia production, Brightspace implementation, delivery support and quality assurance. I also coordinated a completed mixed-methods non-inferiority study comparing the self-directed and instructor-led programmes. A manuscript is in preparation, so this portfolio shows the evaluation design rather than unpublished results.
