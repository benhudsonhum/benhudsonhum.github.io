About

Education, research and making things people can actually use.

I am an instructional designer and education specialist based in Ottawa. For more than a decade, I have worked across healthcare, higher education, the public sector and international development.

My work often begins where specialist knowledge needs to become clear, practical learning: a curriculum, course, simulation, workshop or resource that supports people in the decisions they need to make.

I am most useful when a project needs both careful analysis and hands-on production, with enough structure to remain maintainable after launch.

Between disciplines

My background combines a PhD in theatre, an MEd in teaching and learning, extensive curriculum and facilitation work, and healthcare research. It has made me attentive to performance—not only what learners know, but what they need to notice, decide and do.

Selected experience

  • CHEO Research Institute
    Healthcare instructional design, multimedia simulation, Brightspace implementation and formal evaluation
  • Theatre for a Change
    Curriculum, training and assessment in Malawi and Southern Africa, followed by freelance instructional-design work
  • Refugee Hub / Global Refugee Sponsorship Initiative
    Blended learning developed with an IRCC content specialist and reviewed by UNHCR and IRCC stakeholders
  • University teaching and international training
    Lectures, seminars, practical workshops, facilitation and implementation support
