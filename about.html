About

Education, research and making things people can actually use.

I am an instructional designer and education specialist based in Ottawa. For more than a decade, I have worked across healthcare, higher education, the public sector and international development—usually at the point where specialist knowledge needs to become clear, practical learning.

I work comfortably between disciplines.

My background combines a PhD in theatre, an MEd in teaching and learning, extensive curriculum and facilitation work, and healthcare research. That combination has made me particularly attentive to performance: not only what learners know, but what they need to notice, decide and do.

I work across analysis, design, development, implementation, facilitation and evaluation. The method changes with the context, but the learner's task stays central.

Tools matter when they make the learning work better.

I bring the same approach to digital production. I use eLearning, multimedia, simulation and visual design to make complex practice visible and give learners meaningful ways to rehearse it. I am equally comfortable with the quieter work that makes a programme dependable: testing, documentation, revision and maintenance.

Working approach

Four principles guide the work.

Listen before redesigning

Understand the subject, audience, existing strengths and constraints.

Make the reasoning visible

Design examples, practice and feedback around how competent decisions are made.

Build carefully

Treat accessibility, QA, LMS behaviour and documentation as part of the design.

Use evidence to revise

Combine learner, stakeholder, technical and evaluation evidence rather than relying on taste.
