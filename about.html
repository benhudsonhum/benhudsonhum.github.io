About

Education, research and making things people can actually use.

I am an instructional designer and education specialist based in Ottawa. For more than a decade, I have worked across healthcare, higher education, the public sector and international development.

I work where specialist knowledge needs to become clear, practical learning: a curriculum, course, simulation, workshop or resource that helps people understand, practise and act.

I am most useful when a project needs careful analysis, collaborative design and hands-on production—and when the result must remain usable and maintainable after launch.

I work between disciplines.

My background combines a PhD in theatre, an MEd in teaching and learning, university teaching, curriculum and facilitation work, and healthcare research. That range has made me attentive to performance: not only what learners know, but what they need to notice, decide, practise and do. It has also reinforced a principle that runs through my work: people learn through participation, response and reflection, not simply by receiving information.

Selected experience

  • Research Associate / Instructional Designer · CHEO Research Institute
    ADHD diagnostic-interview learning across self-directed, webinar and simulation formats, with interactive video, Brightspace implementation, project leadership and formal evaluation
  • Curriculum, Training and Assessment Advisor · Theatre for a Change
    Participatory curriculum, facilitator development and multimedia resources in Malawi and Southern Africa, followed by later freelance curriculum design
  • Instructional Designer · Refugee Hub / Global Refugee Sponsorship Initiative
    Blended refugee-sponsorship learning developed with an IRCC content specialist and reviewed by UNHCR and IRCC stakeholders
  • University teaching and international training
