Approach

The learner's task stays in view.

I am most useful when the subject is complicated, stakeholders see it differently and the learning needs to hold up in practice. Research informs the work, but the output must remain clear, usable and possible to maintain.

01

Understand the work

Clarify the audience, context, decisions, constraints and evidence of competent performance.

02

Design for practice

Align outcomes, examples, scenarios, activities, assessment and feedback around what learners must do.

03

Build the system

Develop the learning, multimedia, LMS flow, facilitator resources and documentation as one coherent experience.

04

Test, evaluate and revise

Check accuracy, usability, accessibility, technical behaviour and evidence from learners and stakeholders.
