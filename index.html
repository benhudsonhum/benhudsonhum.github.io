Approach

The learner's task stays in view.

I treat analysis, curriculum, production, implementation and evaluation as parts of the same piece of work.

01

Understand the work

Understand what learners need to do, what they already bring and what may stand in the way.

02

Design for practice

Align outcomes, examples, practice, assessment and feedback with the decisions learners need to make.

03

Build for delivery

Develop the course, media, LMS experience and supporting resources as one coherent whole.

04

Test and revise

Use learner, instructor, stakeholder, technical and evaluation evidence to guide revision.
